  When buyers first hear "17-year lease," many assume it's a rental arrangement
  — that they don't actually own the property. This is the single biggest
  misconception we encounter.


  **You own the property outright.** The 17-year lease refers to the structured
  investment and management framework, not the ownership.


  ## What the 17-Year Lease Actually Is


  The lease is a contractual agreement between you (the owner) and Engrite's
  property management arm. It governs:


  1. **How the property is managed** for the lease term

  2. **What rental income you receive** and how it's calculated

  3. **Maintenance responsibility** allocation

  4. **Exit options** at year 17


  ## Why 17 Years?


  This isn't arbitrary. 17 years aligns with:

  - **Typical Lagos mortgage tenure** (15-20 years) — buyers can finance against
  the asset

  - **Building maintenance cycle** — major renovations needed around year 15-18

  - **Investment horizon for diaspora buyers** — long enough to compound, short
  enough to plan exit


  ## What It Protects Against


  ### Market Volatility

  The lease locks in your return framework. Even if Lagos rental markets dip in
  a given year, your structured return is contractual — Engrite absorbs the
  variance.


  ### Tenant Risk

  For Earn-mode buyers, Engrite handles tenant vetting, lease enforcement, and
  eviction if needed. You never interact with tenants directly.


  ### Maintenance Surprises

  Major maintenance (roof, structural, electrical) is Engrite's responsibility
  during the lease term. You only pay for interior cosmetics.


  ## Exit Options at Year 17


  1. **Renew** — continue under updated terms (typically 5-year renewable)

  2. **Self-manage** — take full control, no Engrite involvement

  3. **Sell back to Engrite** — at pre-agreed minimum valuation formula


  ## For Live-Mode Buyers


  Even if you live in the property yourself, the 17-year lease still applies —
  but in a different form. You receive the structural warranty, maintenance
  coverage, and the resale assurance, without the rental income component. The
  benefit is protection, not yield.


  ## Bottom Line


  The 17-year lease is a capital protection mechanism, not an ownership
  limitation. It's why Engrite buyers have a 100% on-time return rate since 2020
  — even through the 2023 naira devaluation.
