  Lagos real estate delivered a **23% average appreciation** in 2025, according
  to data from the Nigerian Bureau of Statistics and corroborated by independent
  valuations from three Lagos-based firms.


  This outpaced every major Nigerian asset class:


  | Asset Class | 2025 Return |

  |-------------|-------------|

  | Lagos Real Estate | +23.4% |

  | NGN Stock Index | +18.1% |

  | Nigerian Bonds | +14.2% |

  | Naira vs USD | -42.0% |


  ## The Four Structural Drivers


  ### 1. Lagos Population Density

  Lagos adds an estimated 77 people per hour — the fastest population growth of
  any African city. Housing supply cannot keep pace.


  ### 2. Diaspora Capital Inflow

  Nigerians abroad sent home $19.6 billion in remittances in 2025, with a
  growing portion directed at property purchase rather than consumption.


  ### 3. Infrastructure Investment

  The Lekki Deep Sea Port, Red Line rail, and Fourth Mainland Bridge have
  unlocked previously marginal corridors — Gbagada, Ikorodu, and Epe all saw
  outsized gains.


  ### 4. Construction Cost Inflation

  Cement, steel, and labor costs rose 31% in 2025. This means new builds are
  more expensive, lifting the value of existing stock.


  ## What This Means for 2026


  We expect 15-20% continued appreciation in 2026, with the strongest gains in:

  - **Gbagada Phase One** (where Crest Residence is located)

  - **Yaba corridor** (where Sinai Spaces and Sinai Residence sit)

  - **Epe** (long-term play)


  The window for off-plan entry pricing is closing fast. Crest Residence's
  launch pricing will not be available once foundation is complete.
