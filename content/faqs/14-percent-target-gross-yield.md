question: What does the 14% target gross yield mean?
category: investment
order: 7
answer: >-
  The 14% figure is a target gross-yield assumption before management fees,
  vacancy, maintenance, taxes and other ownership costs. For example, a ₦20M
  unit at 14% models ₦2.8M in gross annual rent before those deductions. It is
  not a guaranteed or fixed return. Request the current financial illustration
  for your selected unit and review the lease, payment, reporting and management
  terms before you commit.
