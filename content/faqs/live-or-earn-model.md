question: What exactly is the 'Live or Earn' model?
category: investment
order: 1
answer: >-
  Live or Earn describes two potential use paths: occupy the unit yourself, or
  appoint a property manager to seek tenants and manage rental operations.
  Availability, switching terms, fees, income and the buyer's legal interest
  depend on the project-specific offer and signed agreement.
