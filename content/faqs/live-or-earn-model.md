question: What exactly is the 'Live or Earn' model?
category: investment
order: 1
answer: >-
  Live or Earn is Engrite's signature flexibility clause. When you buy a
  property, you have two options: move in and live in it yourself (Live), or let
  Engrite's property management team lease it out to vetted tenants and remit
  rental income to you (Earn). You can switch between modes — for example, a
  diaspora buyer might Earn for 5 years, then switch to Live when they relocate
  back to Lagos. There is no fee for switching, and you retain full ownership
  throughout.
