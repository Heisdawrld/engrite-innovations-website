question: How is the 7.1% annual return calculated and paid?
category: investment
order: 7
answer: >-
  The 7.1% figure is an illustrative gross-yield projection based on the
  assumptions used for selected units. Actual income can vary with occupancy,
  rent achieved, management fees, maintenance, taxes and market conditions.
  Request the current financial illustration for your chosen unit and review
  the payment, reporting and management terms before you commit. Projected
  returns are not guaranteed.
