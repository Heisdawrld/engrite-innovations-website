question: How is the 7.1% annual return calculated and paid?
category: investment
order: 7
---

The 7.1% return is calculated on your total purchase price and paid quarterly into your nominated bank account (Nigerian or international). It is derived from actual rental income generated by the property — Engrite's property management arm leases the unit, deducts a 15% management fee from rent collected, and remits the remainder to you. Returns are documented in quarterly statements showing rent collected, vacancy periods, maintenance costs, and net payout. You can audit any time.
