question: Are there any restrictions on foreign (non-Nigerian) buyers?
category: diaspora
order: 11
answer: >-
  Eligibility, title structure, required consent and tax treatment depend on
  the buyer, the property and the transaction. Foreign buyers should obtain
  independent Nigerian legal and tax advice before reserving a unit. Engrite
  can provide the project documents and introduce the closing process, but the
  applicable approvals and costs must be confirmed for your specific purchase.
