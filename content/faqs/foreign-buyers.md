question: Are there any restrictions on foreign (non-Nigerian) buyers?
category: diaspora
order: 11
answer: >-
  Foreign nationals can buy property in Lagos subject to the same process as
  Nigerian citizens, with one additional step: obtaining a Minister's Consent
  for land transfer (we handle this as part of the 5% legal fee package).
  Foreign buyers are eligible for the same Live or Earn model, the same 17-year
  lease structure, and the same 7.1% return. Rental income is subject to 10%
  withholding tax, which we deduct and remit on your behalf.
