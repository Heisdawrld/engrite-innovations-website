question: What happens if construction is delayed?
category: legal
order: 9
---

Every Engrite purchase agreement includes a liquidated damages clause: if handover is delayed beyond the committed date by more than 60 days due to Engrite's fault (not force majeure), you receive a daily penalty credit applied to your final payment. We have never invoked force majeure. Our track record: Sinai Spaces delivered on original committed date, Sinai Residence delivered 3 weeks early. We publish monthly progress updates with photos so buyers can verify construction status independently.
