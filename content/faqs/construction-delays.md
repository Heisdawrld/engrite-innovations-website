question: What happens if construction is delayed?
category: legal
order: 9
answer: >-
  The applicable purchase agreement sets out the delivery target, any grace
  period, force-majeure provisions and the remedies available if a delay occurs.
  Review those clauses with your lawyer before signing. Engrite also provides
  dated construction updates so buyers can follow progress and raise questions
  before handover.
