question: Can I visit the construction site before purchasing?
category: property
order: 8
answer: >-
  Yes, and we strongly encourage it for local buyers. Site visits can be booked
  through our booking widget — typically a 90-minute guided tour with a sales
  consultant who walks you through the unit mix, construction quality, and
  neighborhood. For off-plan properties, the team can show the current site,
  available samples and approved project material relevant to the development.
  Diaspora buyers can book virtual site tours via video call.
