question: Can I visit the construction site before purchasing?
category: property
order: 8
---

Yes, and we strongly encourage it for local buyers. Site visits can be booked through our booking widget — typically a 90-minute guided tour with a sales consultant who walks you through the unit mix, construction quality, and neighborhood. For off-plan properties where construction hasn't begun, we'll show you comparable completed projects (Sinai Spaces or Sinai Residence) so you can evaluate Engrite's build quality firsthand. Diaspora buyers can book virtual site tours via video call.
