question: What is the 5% legal fee and what does it cover?
category: legal
order: 6
answer: >-
  The 5% legal fee is the only fee beyond the property purchase price. It is a
  one-time payment that covers: title verification at the Lands Bureau, deed of
  assignment drafting by an independent attorney, notarization, stamp duty,
  Lagos State land registry registration, and the structural warranty
  registration. There are no hidden marketing fees, agency fees, or management
  fees deducted at closing. Ongoing property management (if you choose Earn
  mode) is deducted from rental income, never billed separately.
