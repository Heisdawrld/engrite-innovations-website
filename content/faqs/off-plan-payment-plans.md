question: What are the payment plans for off-plan properties?
category: property
order: 5
---

Off-plan properties (like Crest Residence) follow a milestone-based payment schedule tied to construction progress, not arbitrary dates. A typical schedule is: 10% reservation, 20% at foundation, 30% at superstructure, 25% at finishing, 15% at handover. This means you only pay as the building physically progresses — your capital is never sitting idle. The full schedule is documented in your purchase agreement before any payment is made.
