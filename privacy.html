4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under the NDPA, we rely on the following lawful bases to process your personal data:

  • Consent — when you tick a box, submit a form, or subscribe.
  • Legitimate interest — to operate, secure, and improve our business.
  • Contract — when processing is needed to enter into or perform a contract with you.
  • Legal obligation — when we are required by Nigerian law to retain or disclose information.

We do not sell your personal data. We only share it with:

  • Members of the Engrite Innovations team who need it to serve your request.
  • Third-party service providers we use to operate our business — hosting (Netlify), email, and where applicable, payment and document services — bound by appropriate data-protection terms.

6. How Long We Keep It

We keep your personal data only as long as needed to fulfil the purpose we collected it for, to comply with our legal obligations, or to resolve disputes. Marketing emails are sent until you unsubscribe. Enquiry data is typically retained for up to 3 years from your last interaction with us.

7. Your Rights Under the NDPA

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct anything that is wrong or out of date.
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your personal data, subject to our legal obligations.
  • Restriction — ask us to stop using your data while we resolve a concern.
  • Objection — object to direct marketing or other processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, with no effect on processing already done before withdrawal.
  • Complain — to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) at ndpc.gov.ng if you believe we have mishandled your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at engriteinnovations@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children
